# HealthVault User Guide

Welcome to HealthVault! This guide will help you make the most of your digital health management system.

## 📱 Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Visit the HealthVault homepage
2. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign Up"**
3. Fill in your information:
   - **Full Name**: Your complete legal name
   - **Email**: A valid email address for login
   - **Phone**: (Optional) Your contact number
   - **Password**: At least 6 characters
4. Click **"Create Health ID"**
5. Your unique Health ID will be generated automatically (e.g., HV-ABC123-XYZ456)

### Signing In

1. Click **"Sign In"** from the homepage
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **"Sign In"**

## 🏠 Dashboard

Your dashboard is your health command center.

### What You'll See:
- **Health ID**: Your unique identifier (keep this safe!)
- **Quick Stats**: 
  - Total medical records
  - Active reminders
  - Emergency status
- **Recent Records**: Last 5 uploaded documents
- **Upcoming Reminders**: Next 5 scheduled reminders
- **Quick Actions**: Fast access to common tasks

## 📄 Managing Medical Records

### Uploading a Record

1. Go to **"Medical Records"** from the sidebar
2. Click **"Upload Record"**
3. Choose a category:
   - **Prescription**: Medicine prescriptions from doctors
   - **Lab Report**: Blood tests, urine tests, etc.
   - **Vaccination**: Immunization records
   - **Scan**: X-rays, MRI, CT scans, ultrasound
   - **Other**: Any other medical document
4. Enter a **title** (e.g., "Blood Test - Jan 2026")
5. Add a **description** (optional - notes about the record)
6. Choose your file (PDF, JPG, or PNG, max 10MB)
7. Toggle **"Visible in Emergency Card"** if this should be accessible via QR
8. Click **"Upload"**

### Finding Your Records

- **Search**: Type keywords in the search box
- **Filter**: Use the category dropdown to filter by type
- **View**: Click the eye icon to open the document
- **Download**: Click the download icon to save to your device
- **Delete**: Click the trash icon to permanently remove

### Best Practices

✅ **DO:**
- Use clear, descriptive titles (include date and type)
- Add descriptions with important notes
- Upload high-quality, readable images
- Keep prescriptions and lab reports organized

❌ **DON'T:**
- Upload unrelated personal documents
- Use vague titles like "Document 1"
- Upload files larger than 10MB
- Share your account credentials

## 🚨 Emergency Card

Your emergency card provides critical health information during emergencies.

### Setting Up Your Emergency Profile

1. Go to **"Emergency Card"** from the sidebar
2. Click **"Edit"**
3. Fill in all relevant information:
   - **Blood Group**: A+, A-, B+, B-, O+, O-, AB+, AB-
   - **Allergies**: List all known allergies (medicines, food, etc.)
   - **Medical Conditions**: Chronic conditions (diabetes, hypertension, etc.)
   - **Emergency Contact Name**: Someone to call in emergencies
   - **Emergency Contact Phone**: Their phone number
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Using Your QR Code

1. After setting up your profile, you'll see a QR code
2. Click **"Download QR Code"** to save it as an image
3. Print the QR code
4. Place it in:
   - Your wallet
   - Car glove compartment
   - Gym bag
   - Home emergency kit
   - Office desk

### How It Works

- Anyone can scan your QR code with their phone camera
- They'll see ONLY your emergency information
- They CANNOT access your full medical records
- No login required for emergency access
- Critical information is instantly available to first responders

### What's Visible via QR?
✅ Blood group  
✅ Allergies  
✅ Medical conditions  
✅ Emergency contact details  

❌ NOT visible: Full medical records, prescriptions, lab reports

## ⏰ Reminders

Never miss a medicine dose or doctor appointment!

### Creating a Reminder

1. Go to **"Reminders"** from the sidebar
2. Click **"Add Reminder"**
3. Choose the type:
   - **Medicine**: For medication schedules
   - **Appointment**: For doctor visits
4. Enter details:
   - **Title**: What is this reminder for?
   - **Description**: Additional notes (dosage, doctor name, etc.)
   - **Date**: When should you be reminded?
   - **Time**: What time?
   - **Frequency**:
     - **Once**: Single occurrence
     - **Daily**: Every day at the same time
     - **Weekly**: Once a week
     - **Monthly**: Once a month
5. Click **"Create Reminder"**

### Managing Reminders

- **Mark Complete**: Click the bell icon to mark as done
- **View**: Toggle between "Active" and "Completed" tabs
- **Delete**: Click the trash icon to remove

### Overdue Reminders

- Overdue reminders appear in red
- You'll see an "Overdue" label
- A banner shows total overdue count

## ⚙️ Settings

### Updating Your Profile

1. Go to **"Settings"** from the sidebar
2. Update your:
   - Full name
   - Phone number
3. Click **"Save Changes"**

**Note**: Email and Health ID cannot be changed.

### Deleting Your Account

⚠️ **WARNING**: This action is permanent and cannot be undone!

1. Go to **"Settings"**
2. Scroll to **"Danger Zone"**
3. Click **"Delete Account"**
4. Confirm twice (all data will be deleted)

What gets deleted:
- Your profile and Health ID
- All medical records and files
- Emergency profile
- All reminders
- Account login

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

### Your Data Privacy

- **Your data belongs to you**: You control what information is stored
- **Secure storage**: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Access control**: Only you can access your full medical records
- **Emergency sharing**: You choose what's visible via QR code

### Keeping Your Account Safe

✅ **Best Practices:**
- Use a strong, unique password
- Don't share your login credentials
- Log out on shared devices
- Keep your QR code private (but accessible for emergencies)
- Review your records regularly
- Update emergency information when it changes

❌ **Avoid:**
- Using the same password as other websites
- Sharing your Health ID publicly online
- Leaving your account logged in on public computers
- Uploading sensitive information you don't want in emergencies

## 💡 Tips & Tricks

### Organize Your Records

- Upload records immediately after receiving them
- Use consistent naming: "Type - Date - Details"
  - ✅ "Lab Report - Jan 2026 - Blood Test"
  - ❌ "scan.pdf"
- Add descriptions with key findings
- Mark important records for emergency visibility

### Stay on Top of Reminders

- Set reminders as soon as prescriptions are written
- Add appointment reminders when booking
- Use daily reminders for regular medications
- Review and complete reminders daily

### Keep Emergency Info Current

- Update allergies immediately if new ones are discovered
- Add new medical conditions when diagnosed
- Keep emergency contact information up to date
- Review emergency profile every 6 months

### Regular Maintenance

**Monthly:**
- Upload any new medical documents
- Review upcoming appointments
- Check for outdated reminders

**Quarterly:**
- Update emergency profile if needed
- Review and organize medical records
- Print a fresh QR code if damaged

**Annually:**
- Verify all information is current
- Delete very old, irrelevant records
- Update emergency contact if changed

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Can't Upload a File?

- **Check file size**: Must be under 10MB
- **Check format**: Only PDF, JPG, PNG allowed
- **Check internet**: Ensure stable connection
- **Try smaller file**: Compress or resize if needed

### QR Code Not Working?

- Ensure emergency profile is filled out
- Try downloading QR code again
- Use a different QR scanner app
- Check if image is clear and not damaged

### Reminder Not Showing?

- Check if you're on "Active" tab (not "Completed")
- Verify the date is today or future
- Refresh the page
- Check if accidentally marked as complete

### Forgot Password?

Currently, password reset must be done through Supabase dashboard. Contact support for assistance.
